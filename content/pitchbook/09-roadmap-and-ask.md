# Roadmap & The Ask

## Where Ten&See goes from here

### Roadmap

**Phase 1 — Foundation (now)**
- Live beta platform + TSEF operating system
- Active lead pipeline and agent network at D'Latour, DK, and surrounding buildings
- First closed deals with the 10% success-fee model proven end-to-end

**Phase 2 — Liquidity (next 2 quarters)**
- 50+ verified units across the beachhead university cluster
- Repeatable agent onboarding (commission agreement signed, inventory logged)
- Marketing engine: university partnerships, student-community channels, referral loops
- Graph database live — every entity queryable, matching semi-automated

**Phase 3 — Intelligence (quarters 3–4)**
- Department AI agents live: auto-matching, auto-follow-up, self-writing finance reports
- Second university cluster opened, chosen from graph data
- Recurring revenue from renewals and repeat agent relationships

**Phase 4 — Scale (month 12+)**
- Multi-cluster coverage in the Klang Valley; first city expansion
- Marketplace features: featured listings, verified-agent tier
- The self-optimizing company: expansion planning driven by the Strategy Agent

### The ask

Ten&See is raising a pre-seed round to fund:

1. **Supply acquisition** — agent onboarding and verified-listing coverage of the beachhead cluster
2. **Demand generation** — student-facing marketing at semester-intake moments
3. **Intelligence layer build-out** — the graph + agent infrastructure that makes the company compound
4. **Founder runway** — full-time focus for the three-person team

### Why now

Semester intakes are fixed clocks — every month of delay is a cohort of students housed the old, broken way. The platform is live, the pipeline is real, and the operating system is already running. **We are not asking for money to start. We are asking for fuel to accelerate.**
