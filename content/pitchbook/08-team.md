# The Team

## Three founders, six departments, one operating system

Ten&See is built by a three-person founding team that lives inside its own market — students in Malaysia who experienced the housing problem firsthand.

### Founders

| | Role | Owns |
|---|---|---|
| **Dhanyal (Danny) Mohammad** | Product & Technology | Platform, infrastructure, AI layer, deployment |
| **Marwa Mozammal** | Marketing & Growth | Brand, channels, partnerships, lead generation |
| **Ansar Alam Mohammed** | Operations & Administration | Process, decisions, coordination, documentation |

Sales & Business Development, Finance & Strategy, and Technical Support are run as shared intelligence loops across the three founders until dedicated hires are justified by deal volume.

### How a three-person team runs six departments

- **Departments are loops, not headcount.** Each department is a SENSE → THINK → ACT → LEARN cycle defined in the Master Operations Document — executable by any founder, and progressively by AI agents
- **Written operating system.** Every meeting produces decisions with owners and deadlines; every week produces finance, sales, and tech reports; everything is logged in TSEF
- **AI leverage.** The founding principle — humans are triggers, AI is the executor — means the team's output scales far beyond its size

### Founder-market fit

- We are the customer: international students navigating Malaysian student housing
- We have the supply relationships: direct WhatsApp lines to agents covering the key buildings
- We build the product ourselves: full-stack development, AI-native tooling, no outsourcing dependency
