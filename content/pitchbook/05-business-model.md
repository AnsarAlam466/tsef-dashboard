# Business Model

## Success fees on closed deals — aligned incentives, zero inventory risk

### Core revenue: the 10% success fee

Ten&See charges a **10% success fee** on closed rentals, taken from the apartment/landlord side. The student pays nothing extra.

**Worked example — a real deal shape from our pipeline:**

| | |
|---|---|
| 2 students take a whole 2-person unit at D'Latour | RM1,500/person |
| Total monthly rent | RM3,000 |
| Ten&See success fee (10%) | **RM300 per closing** |

### Why this model works

- **No inventory risk** — we never lease or hold units; we monetize the match
- **Aligned incentives** — we earn only when a student is actually housed
- **Agent-friendly** — agents keep their commission structure; we bring them demand they didn't have
- **Scales with the graph** — each new agent adds supply, each new student adds demand, and matching cost approaches zero as the system automates

### Unit economics discipline

Finance tracks, per the operating rhythm:

- Revenue per listing, per building, per university market
- Cost per lead by channel (organic, referral, campaigns)
- Close rate and time-to-close per agent
- LTV per landlord/agent relationship (repeat closings)

### Future revenue layers (roadmap, not promises)

1. **Featured listings** — landlords pay for placement once marketplace liquidity exists
2. **Verified-agent subscriptions** — tooling and demand access for high-volume agents
3. **Student services** — move-in bundles, deposits, renewals
4. **Data products** — occupancy and pricing intelligence for property operators
