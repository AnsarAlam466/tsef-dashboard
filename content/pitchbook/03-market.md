# The Market

## Malaysia: a structural, semester-driven housing demand engine

### Why Malaysia

- Malaysia hosts one of Southeast Asia's largest international student populations, drawn by affordable, English-language education and globally recognized universities
- International students **must** find private housing — most universities offer limited on-campus capacity
- Demand renews **every semester**, forever. This is not a one-time market; it is a recurring pipeline

### Beachhead: university clusters in the Klang Valley

We start where we live and study — the university corridors around Kuala Lumpur (Taylor's, Sunway, Monash, APU, UCSI and neighboring campuses), focusing on high-density student buildings such as **D'Latour** and **DK** where we already have agent relationships and live student demand.

### Segments

| Segment | Behavior | Our angle |
|---|---|---|
| International students (new intake) | Highest urgency, least local knowledge | Verified listings + guided matching |
| Returning students | Know the buildings, want better deals | Price transparency + fast availability |
| Groups of friends | Want whole units to split | Group matching (e.g., 2-person unit ÷ 2) |

### Expansion path

1. **Deepen** — more buildings and agents around the beachhead universities
2. **Widen** — additional Klang Valley university clusters
3. **Replicate** — other Malaysian education hubs (Cyberjaya, Penang, Johor), then regional markets with the same structure

The playbook is data-driven: the graph tells us which university cluster to open next, based on lead volume and close rates — not guesswork.
